This is not a test or a judgement. It is written in everyday terms, about what the owner experiences and what you do at the office. No technical knowledge needed, and it takes about 20 to 30 minutes. "Partial" and "Not yet" are completely normal for a platform still being built. The more honest the picture, the better the roadmap.

  • Live: it works for real owners today.
  • Partial: started, or it looks done but is not fully working or reliable yet.
  • Not yet: it does not exist yet.
  • Not sure: not certain, or it does not apply. Just say so in the notes.
