What we do

We help good ideas become real things.

Most ideas die somewhere between the first sketch and the day they have to stand on their own. That gap is where we are useful. Sometimes we are the ones building; sometimes the idea is yours and we are simply there to help it across.

One helping hand, the whole way from idea to the thing actually working.

01 Build

Some ideas we take on ourselves.

We start the company, ship the product, and run it until it can stand without us. The projects below are ours, or partly ours, and most of them are still being built right now.

02 Back

Some ideas belong to someone else.

We put in money, time and the scars of having done it before, and we stay involved where that actually helps. Not a name on a cap table; someone you can call when something breaks.

03 Coach

Some founders just need a hand.

So we sit next to them, week after week, on the real decisions and the boring ones, until the plan turns into something that holds up. No reports, no theatre; just the work.

How we work

It starts with a conversation, not a pitch deck.

Someone has an idea, or a mess, or a half-built thing that stalled. From there, the helping hand works roughly like this.

We listen first

We ask the awkward questions, and together we work out whether we can actually help. If we cannot, we say so, and point you somewhere better.

Then hands get dirty

We are not the kind of help that writes a report and leaves. We stay close to what decides whether something lives: the product, the customers, the money.

We build, or step back

When it helps to build, we build. When it helps to let you lead, we step back. The point is to get the thing standing on its own feet.

And we speak plainly

If something is not working, you hear it from us early, while it is still cheap to fix. If it is working, we help you push.

The short version

No jargon, no posturing, no theatre. Just an honest hand on the work, for as long as the work needs it.
