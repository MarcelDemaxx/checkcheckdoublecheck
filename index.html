What we do

We help good ideas become real things.

Most ideas die somewhere between the first sketch and the day they have to stand on their own. That gap is where we are useful. Sometimes we are the ones building; sometimes the idea is yours and we are simply there to help it across.

One helping hand, the whole way from idea to the thing actually working.

01 Build

Some ideas we take on ourselves.

We start the company, ship the product, and run it until it can stand without us. The projects below are ours, or partly ours, and most of them are still being built right now.

02 Back

Some ideas belong to someone else.

We put in money, time and the scars of having done it before, and we stay involved where that actually helps. Not a name on a cap table; someone you can call when something breaks.

03 Coach

Some founders just need a hand.

So we sit next to them, week after week, on the real decisions and the boring ones, until the plan turns into something that holds up. No reports, no theatre; just the work.

How we work

It starts with a conversation, not a pitch deck.

Someone has an idea, or a mess, or a half-built thing that stalled. We listen, we ask the awkward questions, and together we work out whether we can actually help. If we cannot, we will say so, and point you somewhere better.

Then we get our hands dirty. We are not the kind of help that writes a report and leaves. We stay close to the work that decides whether something lives: the product, the customers, the money, and the unglamorous parts in between.

When it helps to build, we build. When it helps to step back and let you lead, we step back. The point is never to make ourselves look important; it is to get the thing standing on its own feet.

And we say what we think, plainly. If something is not working, you will hear it from us early, while it is still cheap to fix. If it is working, we will help you push. No jargon, no posturing; just an honest hand on the work.

The work

What idea to realisation looks like in practice.

These are the projects on our table at the moment. They are here as proof, not as a catalogue; a few honest examples of the kinds of things we build, back and help across the line.

CCDC

Live

Verifies documents and transactions so parties can trust each other without having to check everything themselves. For companies with mandatory checks, and for people making a large purchase.

CCDC Real Estate

Andalusia pilot

The real estate version of CCDC, for buying and selling in Spain. Agent, buyer, seller and lawyers in one dossier, from the first offer to the escritura.

onlineCIO

In development

Information management, business process management and IT direction, offered as a service to school boards in foundational education. For boards without an information manager of their own.

thnx

Live

Smart tags for lost things. The finder scans, the owner gets a message. For schools and their community, and for anyone who wants their things back. It also brings in money for a good cause or the school community.

In and Out

Sotogrande

A local media and events platform that connects entrepreneurs, residents and visitors in an area, through a curated guide, events and its own magazine. The first location is Sotogrande.

BiedadviesApp

Live

A backoffice and mobility platform for car dealers, from buying at auctions to selling. It gives them grip on the whole business from one place, at the office or on the road.

Different sectors, one way of working: walk it across, step by step. Each of these started as someone's idea and is being walked, step by step, toward something real.

Before this

None of this is a first attempt. There is a long run of building underneath it.

Before all of this, we built a software platform for the transport training market in the Netherlands; two products that together served the whole chain. Opleidingsplatform.nl connected large staffing organisations and transport companies with the people who train drivers.

Autofox served the other side of that market: the transport trainers themselves, including the largest in the country. Close to two decades of entrepreneurship, product development and growth. That experience now sits underneath everything we do here.
